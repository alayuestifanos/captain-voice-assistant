---
id: fuel-bunkering
title: Fuel Bunkering Operations
tags: [engine, fuel, bunkering, pollution]
---

Before bunkering, a pre-transfer meeting between ship and barge or terminal
representatives must confirm the bunker plan, maximum transfer rate,
emergency stop procedures, and communication method. All scuppers must be
plugged and drip trays placed under hose connections to prevent any spill
reaching the sea. The Shipboard Oil Pollution Emergency Plan and spill
response equipment must be readily available on deck throughout the
operation. Tank soundings must be taken and logged before, during (at
intervals), and after bunkering to track quantity received and detect any
discrepancy early. The delivered fuel's Bunker Delivery Note must be
checked against the order, and a representative sample must be drawn and
sealed in the presence of both parties for later analysis. Bunkering must
be stopped immediately if a spill occurs, or if wind, sea state, or other
conditions make the operation unsafe, and the emergency shutdown procedure
must be tested prior to each operation.
