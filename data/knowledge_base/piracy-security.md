title: Piracy and Armed Robbery Precautions
tags: [security, isps, piracy]
---

In High Risk Areas, the ship must implement Best Management Practices,
including increased freeboard measures, razor wire, water spray, and
lighting of vulnerable areas at night. Extra lookouts must be posted with
communication to the bridge, and the Ship Security Alert System (SSAS)
tested before entering the area. A citadel or safe muster point should be
identified and briefed to all crew in case of boarding. If suspicious craft
are sighted approaching, the Captain must be informed immediately, speed
increased if possible, and evasive manoeuvring considered while remaining
within safe navigation limits. UKMTO and other regional reporting centers
should be contacted with position, course, and speed. If pirates succeed in
boarding, crew should proceed to the citadel if briefed to do so, and no
crew member should attempt to resist armed intruders. All security
incidents must be reported per the Ship Security Plan and logged in the
Continuous Synopsis Record.
