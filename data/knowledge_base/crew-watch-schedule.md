title: Crew Hours of Rest and Watch Scheduling
tags: [crew, fatigue, watchkeeping]
---

Under the Maritime Labour Convention and STCW requirements, seafarers must
be given a minimum of 10 hours of rest in any 24-hour period, and 77 hours
in any 7-day period, with rest divisible into no more than two periods, one
of which must be at least 6 hours. Watch schedules must be posted in a
place accessible to all crew, in a working language of the ship and in
English. Records of daily hours of rest must be maintained for each
seafarer and countersigned periodically by the Master. Exceptions for
drills, emergencies, or overriding operational conditions are permitted
but must be compensated with equivalent rest as soon as practicable and
noted in the records. The Master is responsible for ensuring watch
schedules do not induce fatigue, particularly during periods of heavy
port rotation or extended manoeuvring, and must adjust the schedule when
fatigue risk is identified.
