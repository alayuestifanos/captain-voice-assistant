title: Engine Room Watchkeeping
tags: [engine, machinery, watchkeeping]
---

The Engineer Officer of the Watch (EOOW) is responsible for the safe and
efficient operation of all machinery under their charge. Rounds of the
engine room, steering gear compartment, and machinery spaces must be
carried out at regular intervals, checking for abnormal noise, vibration,
leaks, temperature, or pressure readings outside normal parameters. All
critical alarms must be responded to immediately, and unresolved alarms
escalated to the Chief Engineer. Before any maintenance that takes
essential machinery out of service, the bridge must be informed and
permission obtained, especially for main engine, steering gear, or
generator work. A properly completed engine room log must record fuel,
lube oil, and freshwater consumption, running hours, and any abnormal
events each watch. The EOOW must not leave the engine control room
unattended during manoeuvring periods, restricted waters, or heavy traffic
without proper relief.
