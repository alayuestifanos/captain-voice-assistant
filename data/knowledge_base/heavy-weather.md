id: heavy-weather
title: Heavy Weather Precautions
tags: [navigation, weather, safety]
---

When heavy weather is forecast, the Captain should adjust course and speed
as early as possible to minimize slamming, pounding, and excessive rolling,
consulting weather routing services where available. All loose equipment on
deck and in the accommodation must be secured, and additional lashings
applied to cargo, containers, and deck equipment. Watertight doors,
portholes, and ventilators exposed to weather must be closed and checked.
Fuel and ballast tanks should be kept as full or as empty as practical to
reduce free surface effect on stability. Crew movement on open decks should
be restricted, and any necessary outside work must be done in pairs with a
lifeline and harness. The engine room should be alerted to prepare for
increased engine and generator load and possible reduced RPM. Regular
position reports and weather updates should be logged, and the Company
informed of any significant course or speed deviation taken for safety.
