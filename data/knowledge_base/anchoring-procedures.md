id: anchoring-procedures
title: Anchoring Procedures
tags: [navigation, mooring, anchor]
---

Before anchoring, the OOW must select a suitable anchorage considering
depth, holding ground, swinging room, weather, and traffic, and inform the
engine room and forecastle party in advance. The general rule of thumb for
scope is to veer three to five times the depth of water on chain, and
higher in strong wind or current. The anchor is walked back or let go
under control, never allowed to run out freely except in emergencies, to
avoid damaging the windlass or chain locker. Once the anchor is holding,
an anchor watch must be maintained, taking bearings or GPS position fixes
at regular intervals to detect dragging. An anchor ball (or all-round white
light at night) must be displayed. Before weighing anchor, the bridge
confirms clear water astern and ahead, and the amount of chain recovered is
reported continuously to the bridge until the anchor is aweigh and secured.
