id: mooring-procedures
title: Mooring Operations Safety
tags: [mooring, port, safety]
---

Mooring stations must be briefed before arrival on the mooring plan,
including which lines go out first, the use of tugs, and any local
requirements. Personnel must stay clear of the "snap-back zones" where
mooring lines under tension could recoil dangerously if they part. Proper
personal protective equipment, including hard hats, gloves, and safety
footwear, is mandatory for all mooring party members. Communication
between the bridge, mooring stations, and tugs should be continuous over
handheld VHF radios. Winches must never be operated by anyone other than
trained personnel, and lines should be rendered under control rather than
allowed to run free. Once alongside, lines are adjusted for tide and cargo
operations, and mooring lines are inspected periodically throughout the
port stay for wear, chafing, or excessive tension, especially in way of
fairleads and bollards.
