---
id: man-overboard
title: Man Overboard Procedure
tags: [emergency, safety, drill]
---

The first person to see a person fall overboard must immediately shout "Man
Overboard" and throw the nearest lifebuoy with self-igniting light and
smoke signal toward the person, then keep pointing at them without
interruption. The bridge team executes the Williamson Turn or a single
turn maneuver to bring the ship back along its own track. Engines are
placed on standby, general emergency alarm is sounded (three long blasts
plus the ship's whistle), and a MOB position is marked on GPS immediately.
The rescue boat crew must muster at their station within minutes of the
alarm. The Captain must be notified at once and takes command of the
recovery operation. A distress message is broadcast on VHF Channel 16 if
recovery is not immediate. All man overboard drills must be logged, and
crew must be trained to launch the rescue boat within five minutes in calm
conditions.
