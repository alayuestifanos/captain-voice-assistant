---
id: abandon-ship
title: Abandon Ship Procedure
tags: [emergency, safety, lifeboats]
---

Abandon ship is ordered by the Captain only, and only when the ship's
safety can no longer be assured by other means. The signal is more than
six short blasts followed by one long blast on the ship's whistle and
general alarm, repeated with a verbal announcement over the public address
system. Crew and passengers muster at their designated lifeboat stations
wearing warm clothing and lifejackets, bringing the ship's EPIRB and
portable VHF if safe to do so. Lifeboats and liferafts are launched only on
the Captain's order, prioritizing the side away from wind and list. A
headcount is taken before and after boarding each survival craft, and the
result is reported to the Captain or senior surviving officer. Once clear
of the vessel, survival craft should stay together where possible to aid
detection and rescue, and the EPIRB should be activated to alert search and
rescue authorities.
