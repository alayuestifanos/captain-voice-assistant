id: bridge-watchkeeping
title: Bridge Watchkeeping Standards
tags: [navigation, safety, bridge, watchkeeping]
---

The Officer of the Watch (OOW) is responsible for the safe navigation of the
ship at all times while on duty. A proper lookout must be maintained at all
times by sight, hearing, and all other available means, in order to make a
full appraisal of the situation and the risk of collision. The OOW must not
leave the bridge unattended, even for a short period, unless properly
relieved. Standing orders from the Captain must be read and signed by every
watchkeeping officer before assuming a watch. Watch handover must include a
review of position, course, speed, traffic, weather, and any standing or
night orders. On vessels with an integrated bridge system, the OOW should
periodically cross-check GPS position against radar and visual fixes. Any
doubt about the safety of navigation must be reported to the Captain
immediately, regardless of the hour.
