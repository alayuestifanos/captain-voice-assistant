---
id: fire-emergency
title: Fire Emergency Response
tags: [emergency, safety, firefighting]
---

Upon discovering a fire, the finder must raise the alarm immediately by
activating the nearest manual call point and informing the bridge. The
general emergency alarm (seven short blasts followed by one long blast) is
sounded to muster the crew. The fire party dons breathing apparatus and
fire-resistant clothing before entering the affected space. Boundary cooling
of adjacent bulkheads must begin as soon as possible to prevent fire spread.
Ventilation and fuel supply to the affected space must be secured, and fixed
firefighting systems (CO2, water mist, or foam) may be released only after
a full muster confirms no personnel remain inside. The Captain, as the
on-scene commander or delegate, coordinates all firefighting efforts and
decides when to release fixed systems. All crew must know their fire and
emergency station assignments from the muster list posted throughout the
accommodation.
