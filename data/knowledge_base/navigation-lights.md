id: navigation-lights
title: Navigation Lights and Shapes
tags: [navigation, colregs, lights]
---

All vessels underway between sunset and sunrise, and during restricted
visibility, must display the navigation lights required by the International
Regulations for Preventing Collisions at Sea (COLREGs). A power-driven
vessel underway must show a masthead light forward, a second masthead light
abaft and higher than the forward one (if over 50 meters), sidelights (green
starboard, red port), and a sternlight. A vessel at anchor must show an
all-round white light forward, and a second aft if longer than 100 meters,
plus black ball shapes by day. Vessels not under command display two
all-round red lights in a vertical line. Deck officers must verify that all
navigation lights are tested and logged as operational before departure and
at the start of each watch during hours of darkness.
