id: medical-emergency
title: Onboard Medical Emergency Response
tags: [medical, emergency, crew-welfare]
---

The designated ship's medical officer or trained crew member must assess
and stabilize any injured or ill person using the ship's medical guide and
onboard medical chest. For serious cases, Telemedical Assistance Service
(TMAS) should be contacted via satellite or radio for guidance from a
qualified physician ashore, providing full details of symptoms, vital
signs, and treatment given. The Captain must decide, in consultation with
TMAS, whether the vessel should divert to the nearest port with adequate
medical facilities or whether a medical evacuation by helicopter is
required. All medications administered, dosages, and the patient's
condition over time must be logged. The medical chest inventory must be
checked and replenished at each opportunity, with controlled drugs stored
securely and their use recorded separately. Crew members trained in first
aid and medical care must hold valid STCW medical certificates appropriate
to their role.
