---
id: pilot-boarding
title: Pilot Boarding Arrangements
tags: [navigation, pilot, port]
---

Pilot boarding arrangements must comply with SOLAS requirements: the pilot
ladder must be rigged so that it is not less than 1.5 meters nor more than
9 meters above the water, secured to strong points, with steps horizontal
and equally spaced. A safety line, lifebuoy with self-igniting light, and
adequate lighting must be ready at the boarding point at night. A
responsible officer must supervise the rigging and use of the pilot
ladder and accompany the pilot to and from the bridge. Where the freeboard
exceeds 9 meters, a combination arrangement with an accommodation ladder
must be used. The ladder must be inspected before each use for worn steps,
frayed ropes, or damaged spreaders, and any defect rectified before the
pilot boards or disembarks. The Master and pilot must exchange essential
information on the ship's manoeuvring characteristics, passage plan, and
any defects affecting navigation upon the pilot's arrival on the bridge.
