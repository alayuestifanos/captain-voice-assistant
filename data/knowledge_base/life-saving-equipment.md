title: Life Saving Appliances Maintenance
tags: [safety, lifeboats, equipment]
---

Lifeboats, liferafts, and rescue boats must be inspected weekly, with
lifeboat engines run ahead and astern for a minimum period to confirm
operability. Monthly inspections must include a check of all launching
appliances, on-load release gear, and davit lubrication, recorded in the
ship's logbook. Liferafts must be serviced ashore at approved stations at
intervals specified by the manufacturer, typically annually. Lifejackets
and immersion suits must be checked for damage and correct stowage, with a
sufficient number available for all persons on board plus spares. Free-fall
lifeboat drills and lowering of rescue boats must be conducted per the
statutory drill schedule, at least once every three months for lowering
into the water, with results recorded. Any defect in life-saving
equipment that cannot be immediately rectified must be reported to the
Company and flag state as required, with compensating measures put in
place.
