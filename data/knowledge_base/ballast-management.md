id: ballast-management
title: Ballast Water Management
tags: [engine, environment, ballast]
---

Ballast water must be managed in accordance with the ship's approved
Ballast Water Management Plan and applicable international and port state
requirements. Ballast exchange or treatment must be recorded in the Ballast
Water Record Book immediately after each operation, including position,
volume, and method used. Ships fitted with a Ballast Water Treatment
System must ensure it is operating correctly and that treated water meets
the required discharge standard before release. Ballast operations must be
coordinated with the cargo plan to maintain adequate stability, trim, and
stress within the limits of the loading computer at all times. Officers
must never deballast in port waters where local regulations prohibit
discharge without treatment. Any failure of the ballast treatment system
must be reported to the Company and logged, with contingency measures
followed as per the management plan.
