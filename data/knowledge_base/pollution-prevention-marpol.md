title: Pollution Prevention (MARPOL)
tags: [environment, marpol, pollution]
---

Discharge of oil or oily mixtures is prohibited except under the specific
conditions of MARPOL Annex I, and all such operations must be recorded in
the Oil Record Book at the time they occur. The Oily Water Separator must
be operating correctly and its 15 ppm alarm never bypassed; any bypass
pipe found fitted must be reported and removed. Garbage must be segregated
per MARPOL Annex V categories, and a Garbage Record Book maintained for
all disposal or discharge, noting that plastics must never be discharged
into the sea under any circumstances. Sulphur content of fuel oil in use
must comply with the applicable Emission Control Area limits, with a fuel
changeover procedure completed and logged before entering such areas. Any
accidental discharge of oil, noxious liquid substances, or garbage must be
reported immediately to the Captain, the Company, and the nearest coastal
state as required by the Shipboard Oil Pollution Emergency Plan.
