id: cargo-loading
title: Cargo Loading and Stowage
tags: [cargo, stability, loading]
---

Prior to loading, the Chief Officer must prepare a cargo and stability plan
using the approved loading computer, ensuring the vessel remains within
permissible stress, trim, and stability limits throughout loading,
the voyage, and discharge. Cargo must be stowed and secured according to
the Cargo Securing Manual, with lashings inspected before departure and
periodically during heavy weather. Dangerous goods must be stowed in
accordance with the IMDG Code segregation tables, kept clear of
incompatible cargo, and documented on the dangerous goods manifest.
Hatch covers must be verified weathertight before sailing, particularly
after any cargo operation. Draft surveys should be conducted before and
after loading to verify the quantity of cargo loaded against the bills of
lading. Any cargo damage, shortage, or discrepancy noted during loading
must be reported immediately to the Captain and recorded with photographs
where possible.
