---
id: collision-regulations
title: Collision Avoidance Rules of the Road
tags: [navigation, colregs, safety]
---

When two power-driven vessels are on crossing courses so as to involve risk
of collision, the vessel which has the other on her own starboard side must
keep out of the way. In a head-on situation, both vessels must alter course
to starboard so that each passes on the port side of the other. An
overtaking vessel must keep clear of the vessel being overtaken until fully
past and clear. Any action taken to avoid collision must be positive, made
in ample time, and readily apparent to the other vessel — small or late
alterations of course must be avoided. If in any doubt, reduce speed or stop
engines. A vessel that is the "stand-on" vessel must maintain course and
speed but is obliged to take avoiding action if it becomes apparent that the
give-way vessel is not taking appropriate action. Sound signals (one short
blast for turning to starboard, two for port, five or more for danger) must
be used in restricted visibility and close-quarters situations.
