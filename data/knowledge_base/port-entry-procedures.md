title: Port Entry and Arrival Procedures
tags: [port, navigation, documentation]
---

Prior to arrival, the agent and port authorities must be given advance
notice per local requirements, typically 24 to 72 hours, including ETA,
crew list, cargo manifest, and any health declarations. The Master must
ensure the passage and pilotage plan for the approach is prepared,
reviewed with the bridge team, and includes contingency options and
abort points. Under-keel clearance must be verified against the latest
tide tables and squat calculations for the vessel's draft and speed.
Required documentation, including certificates, crew list, and last port
clearance, must be readied for boarding officials such as customs,
immigration, and port state control. Engines and steering gear must be
tested well in advance of arrival, with results logged. The Master should
brief the crew on port-specific security levels (ISPS) and any local
restrictions before arrival, and ensure the gangway watch and access
control are established as soon as the vessel is secured alongside.
