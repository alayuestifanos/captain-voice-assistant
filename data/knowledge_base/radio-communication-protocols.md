title: Radio Communication and Distress Protocols
tags: [communication, gmdss, distress]
---

VHF Channel 16 is the international calling and distress frequency and must
be monitored continuously while at sea unless otherwise directed. A distress
call follows the format "MAYDAY MAYDAY MAYDAY, this is [vessel name] three
times, position, nature of distress, assistance required, number of
persons on board." Urgency messages use "PAN PAN" and safety messages use
"SECURITE," each repeated three times. The GMDSS installation, including
DSC, must be tested regularly and radio logs maintained recording all
distress, urgency, and safety traffic. When acknowledging a distress call
as the nearest vessel, the ship must proceed to assist unless explicitly
released by the coordinating rescue authority or another vessel confirmed
closer. Routine bridge-to-bridge communication for collision avoidance
should use plain, unambiguous language, stating intentions clearly rather
than assuming the other vessel's actions.
