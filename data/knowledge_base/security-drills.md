title: Security and Emergency Drills Schedule
tags: [drills, safety, training, isps]
---

Statutory drills must be conducted at intervals set by SOLAS and the
company's Safety Management System: abandon ship and fire drills within 24
hours of a significant crew change and thereafter at least monthly,
enclosed space entry and rescue drills at least every two months, and
security drills at least every three months per the Ship Security Plan.
Each drill must be planned to cover different scenarios over time, such as
different fire locations or emergency exits, so that crew do not simply
memorize a single routine. Drill records must include date, scenario,
participants, duration, and any deficiencies noted, with corrective action
tracked to closure. New crew members must be given onboard familiarization
covering their emergency duties before the ship sails, or within 24 hours
of joining. The Master or Safety Officer should debrief the crew after
each drill to reinforce lessons learned and update procedures if gaps are
identified.
